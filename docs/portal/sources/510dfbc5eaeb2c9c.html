1# Production flow and machine counter review
2
3## Result
4
5The change uses an explicit per-step quantity ledger and independently tracked machine sources.
6The original transfer-flow plan is consistent: intermediate output becomes downstream input,
7scrap stays at its originating step, and only the final effective step contributes finished
8order output. Raw device totals are not order totals.
9
10The review covered quantity ownership, production/QC stops, routing changes after recording
11output, order/step lock order, raw baselines, reset/retry handling, assignment changes, tenant
12and role boundaries, reconciliation, migration rollback and operator live updates.

14## Findings corrected
15
16- Removed newest-order/newest-batch guessing from explicit machine counting. Channels require explicit
17 workstation and step assignment; payload hints cannot redirect production.
18- Replaced cache baselines and order-total subtraction with persistent source state, timestamp
19 checks and event-ID deduplication. A reset does not produce guessed output.
20- Guarded multiple good-count sources on one step and wildcard MQTT topics mixing machines.
21- Revalidate source changes, including register type, transforms, topic and transport endpoint.
22 A reset acknowledgement cannot bypass configuration validation. Stale decoder/Modbus transport
23 snapshots are rejected even after new configuration was acknowledged.
24- Retain unapplied/partial readings and reconcile explicitly, with atomic rollback if the target
25 cannot accept the entire remaining good quantity. Preserve original attribution.
26- Reject legacy direct MQTT completion/status writes for transfer orders so they cannot bypass
27 the production workflow's quantity and completion rules.
28- Corrected accessible dropdown names and missing Polish labels found during visible E2E.

49## Release conditions
50
51Existing publishers retain legacy whole-batch counting by default. Migrating a channel to
52explicit counting requires the assignment and timestamp/event-ID contract described in
53[the deployment guide](production-flow-deployment.md). Rehearse migration and peak ingestion
54rate on a production-sized copy; database tests establish correctness, not an ingestion
55capacity or retention budget. Counter evidence is retained and can grow with polling volume.
56Resolve or separately account for the known full-workspace test failures before release.
57
58Use [the browser guide](machine-counter-browser-tests.md) to repeat the functional checks.
59No production deployment or push is part of this session.
